# Cinder Quay

From the ship's chart, choose **Enter the Forge**. Or open `BlacksmithRoom` directly in the Unity Editor.

1. Visit the anvil, workbench, and forge. Press **E** to answer one question at each.
2. Return to the anvil and forge your blade. Walk out the workshop door.
3. Defend the quay from two waves. **Left click** strikes; **hold right click** to guard. An orange flash telegraphs incoming blows. Keep enemies in front of you to block.

Each correct first answer adds 8 damage (16–40 total). Even a rough blade can clear both waves. After a loss, retry with the same blade or reforge with a fresh score.

The cutlass has alternating cuts, a visible guard, and score-dependent finishes. Skeletons rise into the yard, wind up before striking, and fall apart into temporary physics-driven pieces. Sparks, dust, and separate sounds mark swings, blocks, impacts, and hammer blows.

The raid is practice only. It doesn't save grades, award XP, or replace the assigned workshop's repair kit, portal, or server verification. The chart video is still available under **Watch Preview**.
