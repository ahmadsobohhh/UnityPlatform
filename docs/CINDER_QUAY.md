# Cinder Quay

From the ship's chart, choose **Enter the Forge**. Or open `BlacksmithRoom` directly in the Unity Editor.

The practice interior uses the enlarged **Blacksmith's Forge** layout, with Tide Crypt masonry and furniture. A fresh checkout uses bundled workstations and the existing pirate sword. In the Unity Editor, if the sibling project's original prefab and 3DForge dependencies have been imported locally with their metadata, the workshop automatically uses that anvil, hearth, and textured longsword instead. The assigned workshop still uses its existing room and portal.

Three forge hands work in place around a grounded anvil, tool racks, stocked benches, and supply stacks. Walking through the doorway preserves position and facing; the rear shutter slides shut once the player is clear. Dungeon candles sit on stone stands.

The Aurora and Cinder Quay each have a short camera reveal. Press **Space** or **Esc** to skip. On deck, the crew patrols or keeps watch, and the interaction prompt only appears within reach of an unobstructed fixture.

1. Visit the anvil, workbench, and forge. Press **E** to answer one question at each.
2. Return to the anvil, forge your blade, and enter the **Tide Crypt** through the workshop door.
3. Read the rune inscription, explore the side chambers, clear the Grand Ossuary, and solve the vault seal. Cross the Drowned Court and claim the Tidekeeper from the reliquary after the last fight.

**Left click** strikes; **hold right click** to guard. An orange flash telegraphs incoming blows. Keep enemies in front of you to block.

Each correct first answer adds 8 damage (16–40 total). Even a rough blade can clear both waves. After a loss, retry with the same blade or return to the ship.

The first-person glove and sleeve follow the grip of the original forge longsword. The sword has alternating cuts, a visible guard, and score-dependent finishes. Skeletons rise in the crypt, wind up before striking, and fall apart into temporary physics-driven pieces. Sparks, dust, and separate sounds mark swings, blocks, impacts, and hammer blows.

The original Tide Crypt layout is built with selected meshes and the texture atlas from [KayKit – Dungeon Remastered](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Dungeon-Remastered-1.0), revision `b0ca9bd` (CC0; license included with the art). No Skyrim files are used. Raw files from the optional local [Blacksmith's Forge Asset Store pack](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/fantasy/blacksmith-s-forge-17785) are excluded from the public repository. They are not needed to run the quest.

**Back to the ship** works from the pause and result screens. A short animated departure returns you to the Aurora without clearing the selected class.

Aim the reticle directly at a nearby seal or chest to interact. Candles and other scenery are not switches. Optional caches and the relic award gold and XP for the current run only; some caches also restore health. Retry resets the run totals. The raid doesn't save grades, change the classroom wallet, or replace the assigned workshop's repair kit, portal, or server verification. The chart video is still available under **Watch Preview**.
